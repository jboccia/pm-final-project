# Project context for Claude

This repo is an individual final project for Product School's **Product Management** course (Medallia cohort).
Course page (requires login): https://app.productschool.com/courses/pmc/medallia1112

## The project

Take one product from problem to launch across six modules. Each module's in-class lab produces an artifact
committed to this repo. In Module 6, the **Final Project Deliverables Builder** turns those artifacts into
`final-presentation.html`, which is committed and submitted along with the repo URL.

- **Chosen scenario: RouteLogic Velocity (B2B).** Picked because it's the most applicable to the student's business and current role.
- Submit within 7 days of the cohort ending, on the Learning Platform ("Upload Your Product Management
  Foundations Final Project"): repo URL + presentation (GitHub Pages link or HTML file).

### RouteLogic scenario brief

RouteLogic is a logistics platform for regional delivery fleets, built as an all-in-one suite (global shipping
compliance through executive financial reporting). The C-suite sees it as the most powerful logistics OS, but its
growing scope has opened a gap between administrative power and frontline usefulness:

- Sharp decline in usage among the people responsible for **real-time fleet coordination**.
- The **largest enterprise account is trialing a leaner competitor**, calling RouteLogic an administrative burden
  that slows their most time-sensitive work.
- The board worries complexity now hinders the operations it was meant to speed up.
- Years of saying yes have produced specialized features used by a small fraction of customers; for the **95% who
  use it daily**, the extra layers turn a simple job into a struggle.

**Challenge:** strip the distractions and restore core value while keeping high-end enterprise capabilities
available but out of the everyday user's way.

**Goal:** lead the strategy for **RouteLogic Velocity**, an initiative to reclaim operational simplicity, auditing the
current experience with existing user research to decide which features are essential vs. legacy noise, so the
platform proves it can still be the fastest tool on the market.

(with Hypothesis & Metrics in Module 3). Follow the repo template's files, and check with the student when the two disagree.

Each template file says to do the lab in that module's Exercise Guide, then download the `.md` and commit it.
Keep the template's headings and fill in the student's own answers. Don't invent data or numbers: leave
`[placeholders]` and point them out. Update the status checkbox in `README.md` when a deliverable is filled in.

## Tools and workflow

- Labs, drafts, and the prototype all run in the Claude desktop app, Code tab. No other chat or build tools.
- GitHub is used in the browser only. Per the course, Claude opens and merges pull requests; the student doesn't click Merge.
  Still, only open or merge a PR when the student asks for it.

## Grading criteria

Scored 1 Poor (0–49) · 2 Sufficient (50–79) · 3 Excellent (80–100) on:
- **Application of Concepts:** how well the project applies the modules' frameworks and methods.
- **Credibility & Reasoning:** whether choices are justified by evidence and sound reasoning.
- **Clarity:** how clearly the work communicates ideas, findings, and recommendations.
- **Strategic Thinking:** whether it shows a clear vision and real strategic judgment.

When giving feedback on drafts, review against these four criteria.
