## 2. The strategic crisis

_The big-picture business risk. If the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?_

## 3. The moment of misery

_The specific point where the product fails the user, forcing a manual workaround (Google, spreadsheets, group texts, a competitor)._

> The user is currently forced to use external solutions to speed up what RouteLogic is very slow at, export their data for administrative work, or switch to a competitor.

## 4. Problem hook

_One urgent sentence fusing the business risk and the user pain, your pitch for why this is the most important thing to work on right now._

> If we ignore this problem, we risk seeing an even larger decline in usage resulting in more customers exploring other options and ultimately losing customers to competitors.

## 5. Value proposition

_Who it's for, the new value, and why it's urgent to launch now._

> For **customers**, we will **pursue an initiative to reclaim the product's operational simplicity, auditing the current experience with existing user research to decide which features are essential and which are legacy noise, so the platform proves it can still be the fastest tool on the market** because **our biggest account, and potentially others, are at risk**.
